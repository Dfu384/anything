# Iconic landmark video collection

Four real video clips from Wikimedia Commons. Individual credits, original URLs, source titles and licenses are recorded in sources.json and displayed in the game. Attribution and source links are also bundled into PLAY.html.

- Eiffel Tower: Mikani — CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Statue of Liberty: Burkouri — CC0.
- Colosseum: Quahadi — CC BY 4.0.
- Big Ben: Deror avi — CC BY-SA 4.0.

Changes: audio removed, excerpts selected, resized and recompressed to web-compatible H.264 MP4 at 30 fps. Shorter recordings were slowed with motion-interpolated frames to last at least 30 seconds without looping. These are edited real recordings, not 3D renders. The adapted clips retain their respective source licenses, including ShareAlike where applicable. No endorsement by the original authors is implied. Source licenses include their own warranty disclaimers.
